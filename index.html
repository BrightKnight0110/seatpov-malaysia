How to use

  • Drag to look around. Scroll or pinch to zoom like a phone camera.
  • Click any seat to sit there. The panel shows its CAT, section, row and seat.
  • For a specific concert, press New and upload the organiser's seat plan. Set the stage and floor to match it, then paint each section with its CAT.
  • If section numbers sit in the wrong place, use Rotate or Reverse to line them up with the plan.

Models are approximate and built from public figures, not official CAD drawings. Section numbers follow each venue's public scheme, but their exact positions, row counts and seat counts are estimates. Always confirm against the organiser's seating plan before buying.
